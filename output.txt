  QUALITATIVE ANALYSIS (Social Media Context)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────
  FCFS        : Simple, fair order, but long streams block messages.
  SJF         : Best avg wait; notifications/msgs (short) finish fast.
                Risk: uploads/streams (long) may starve.
  Round Robin : Most fair; all tasks get timeslices. Good for live
                streams & interactive notifications.
  Priority    : Critical alerts handled first. Risk of low-priority
                uploads starving if high-priority tasks keep arriving.
